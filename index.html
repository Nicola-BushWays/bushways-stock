Stock list

Every stock item in the catalogue. History shows every change. People on the stock-item list can also Edit a name or pack size, Merge a duplicate into the item it should be, or Delete an item no longer stocked (camp-stock D-007, D-013). Invoices and history already linked to an item always stay with it.

Stock on hand

What a camp holds now and what it's worth, worked out from every receipt — never a number typed in. Damaged units that arrived are counted, and shown so someone can decide about them.

Deleted items

Items taken out of the stock list. Their history stays. A deleted item can be restored; a merged one can't, because everything it held now belongs to the item it was merged into.
